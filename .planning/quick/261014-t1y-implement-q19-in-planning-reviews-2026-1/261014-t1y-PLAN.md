1. **Path and label (Claude's discretion).**
   - A path element is `(box_id, label)`. `box_id` is `str(box["id"])`, or `"?"` if the box has no id.
   - `ValidationResult.path` is `walk.path_ids(path)`: the ids joined by `/` (`obj-1/obj-2`), `""` at the top. Ids are unique per scope, labels are not: minitaur has 5 `p voice-N` boxes with identical inner ids.
   - The message prefix is `walk.path_prefix(path)`: `"[" + " > ".join(labels) + "] "`, and `""` at the top.
   - Label rules (`walk.box_label`):
     - newobj: `box_name` plus the first positional arg (the first `box_args` token that does not start with `@`): `p synth`, `poly~ voice`, `gen~`, `mc.gen~`;

     - any other box: its maxclass;
     - fallback `?`.

   - The top context is the resolved top scope.
   - A `p` inside gen~ or rnbo~ inherits gen or rnbo, matching `Patcher.from_dict` and `test_rnbo_container_and_nested_p`.
